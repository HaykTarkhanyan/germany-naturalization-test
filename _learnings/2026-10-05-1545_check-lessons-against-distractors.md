# Check lesson facts against the wrong options (in both languages), not just the right answer

**Symptom:** My own fact check printed every lesson fact next to its official answer and found nothing. An independent reviewer then found four facts that could still cost points, plus 12 questions I had wrongly labelled "common sense".

**Cause:** A fact can agree with the right answer and *also* agree with a distractor. Checking only fact vs. answer cannot see that. Examples:
- Q70: the fact said the president "appoints the ministers"; distractor C "wählt die Minister aus" is shown in the app's English as "chooses the ministers", so two options looked right. The fix names the difference (ernennt vs. wählt aus).
- Q26: the rule "pick Bundesstaat" also matched the distractor "sozialer und sozialistischer Bundesstaat", and primed "Bundesstaat Deutschland" in Q212 (full name).
- Q104: the lesson listed legal reasons for dismissal but not long illness, which is exactly the believable distractor.
- Q263: "from 14 they are punished" did not rule out "treated like adults".

The "common sense" labels failed the same way: I judged them from memory of the topic instead of asking, for every distractor, "would a newcomer believe this?" (42 "the government makes laws", 297 "Poland", 258 "Jugendamt pays Kindergeld").

**Consequences:** For study material, review each fact against all four options and their translations, and for any rule of thumb in a lesson, test it against every question it could apply to. A fresh-context reviewer found what a self-check could not; worth doing for content that decides exam points.
