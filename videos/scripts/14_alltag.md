- **Voice EN:** Any two adults may live together as a couple, but not Anne, who is thirteen, and Tim, who is twenty-five: {Q245} she is a child. - **Voice EN:** You are an adult at eighteen, {Q246} and parents must respect the decisions of an adult daughter, for example about who she lives with. {Q267} The legal protection of a pregnant woman shortly before and after the birth is Mutterschutz, {Q247} not Elternzeit.
