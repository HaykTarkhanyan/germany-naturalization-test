# Conventions

- `official/` holds unmodified files exactly as downloaded from BAMF. Name dated files `<bamf-name>_<YYYY-MM-DD of BAMF page date>.<ext>`.
- `data/raw/` is the artifact of record from scraping; `data/questions_bayern.*` and `QUESTIONS_AND_ANSWERS_BAYERN.md` are generated by `scripts/build_dataset.py` and never edited by hand.
- Question `id` = Online-Testcenter number for Bayern (1-300 general, 301-310 Bayern). `catalog_number` = number in the official PDF (Bayern questions restart at 1).
- `answer_index` is 0-based; the CSV uses letters A-D.
- Every fact in README.md about fees, dates, or the law carries the date it was checked.
