14 Die DDR

The GDR (East Germany) · Lesson and all exam questions

13 questions · about 1.3 of your 33 exam questions come from this topic · 6 to memorize, 7 common sense

The GDR called itself 'democratic', but it was a one-party dictatorship watched over by the Stasi.

In 60 seconds
  • DDR = Deutsche Demokratische Republik, 1949-1990, founded in the Soviet zone.
  • It belonged to the Warsaw Pact; its economy was a Planwirtschaft.
  • Stasi = Ministerium für Staatssicherheit, the secret service.
  • 17 June 1953: strikes and a popular uprising.
The state
  • DDR = Deutsche Demokratische Republik (GDR), founded in 1949 in the Soviet occupation zone; it existed from 1949 to 1990. Q190 Q174 Q172 Q217
  • Its flag was black-red-gold with hammer, compass and a wreath of grain; its coat of arms is picture 4. Q187 Q209
Bloc and economy
  • In the Cold War the GDR belonged to the Warsaw Pact, the Eastern military alliance. common sense Q202 Q207
  • Its economy was a Planwirtschaft (planned economy). common sense Q203
The Stasi and 1953
  • Stasi = Ministerium für Staatssicherheit, the GDR's secret service, not the Third Reich's: the Stasi existed only in the GDR. Q199 Q208
  • 17 June 1953: nationwide strikes and a popular uprising. West Germany long marked the day as a public holiday. Q186 Q210
