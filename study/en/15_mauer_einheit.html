In 60 seconds
  • The GDR built the Wall in 1961; Berlin stayed divided until 1989.
  • 1989: 'Wir sind das Volk' (Monday demonstrations), the Wende, the Wall opens.
  • 3 October 1990: reunification. 5 eastern states joined the Federal Republic, under chancellor Helmut Kohl.
  • The 5 eastern states: Brandenburg, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Sachsen, Sachsen-Anhalt, Thüringen.
The Wall
  • The GDR built the Wall in Berlin in 1961; from 1961 to 1989 Berlin was divided by a wall. Q151 Q188 Q189 Q193
1989
  • 'Wir sind das Volk' was the call of the Monday demonstrations in 1989 in the GDR. Q166
  • The Wende (autumn 1989): the GDR changed from a dictatorship to a democracy. common sense Q196
  • The Wall was opened for everyone in 1989 (on 9 November). common sense Q191
1990: one country
  • Deutsche Wiedervereinigung (reunification), 1990: the five eastern states joined the Federal Republic. It is celebrated on 3 October, the Tag der Deutschen Einheit. Q228 Q204 Q194 Q218
  • Helmut Kohl is called the 'chancellor of German unity'. Q215
  • Germany has had today's borders since 1990, and with reunification the new states also became part of the EU. common sense Q219 Q205
The five eastern states
  • Brandenburg, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Sachsen, Sachsen-Anhalt, Thüringen. In every 'which state belonged to the GDR?' question exactly one option is on this list; in the list question, pick the list of exactly these five. Q192 Q195 Q197 Q198 Q200 Q201
