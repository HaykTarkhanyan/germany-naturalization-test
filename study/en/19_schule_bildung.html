19 Schule und Bildung

School and education · Lesson and all exam questions

10 questions · about 1.0 of your 33 exam questions come from this topic · 4 to memorize, 6 common sense

School is compulsory in Germany, and home-schooling is not allowed.

In 60 secondsCompulsory schoolBefore schoolLaterAll 10 exam questions of this topic
In 60 seconds
  • Schulpflicht and Anwesenheitspflicht: children must go to school, and the state oversees schools.
  • Parents decide on religious education until the child is 14, and on kindergarten (a place is guaranteed from age 3).
  • University needs the Abitur; adults can get it at an Abendgymnasium. Volkshochschule = further education.
Compulsory school
  • Most children have Schulpflicht (compulsory schooling) and must be present at school (Anwesenheitspflicht). common sense Q95 Q260
  • The state controls the schools because the Grundgesetz makes it its task. Q68
  • Until the child turns 14, the parents decide whether it attends Religionsunterricht (religious education). common sense Q2
Before school
  • The parents decide whether a child goes to kindergarten. From age 3 every child has the right to a kindergarten place. common sense Q242 Q269
Later
  • To study at a university you normally need the Abitur. Adults can catch it up at an Abendgymnasium (evening school). Q244 Q257 Q261
  • The Volkshochschule (VHS) is a place for further education (Weiterbildung), for everyone. common sense Q270
