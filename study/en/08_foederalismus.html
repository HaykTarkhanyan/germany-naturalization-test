08 Bund, Länder und Gemeinden

Federal state, Länder and municipalities · Lesson and all exam questions

Germany has 16 states with their own governments, and school is their business, not Berlin's.

In 60 secondsThe LänderThree levelsGetting involvedAll 13 exam questions of this topic
In 60 seconds
  • 16 Bundesländer, each with its own government, usually led by a Ministerpräsident.
  • Three levels: Bund, Länder, Kommunen. The lowest is the Gemeinde, led by a Bürgermeister.
  • School policy is a job of the Länder.
  • You can take part locally: citizens' initiatives, volunteering.
The Länder
  • Germany has 16 Bundesländer. Elsass-Lothringen (Alsace-Lorraine) is not one of them; it is in France. Q24 Q25
  • Every Land has its own government, mostly headed by a Ministerpräsident. Electing a Land parliament is a Landtagswahl. Q39 Q37 Q62
  • School policy (Schulpolitik) is decided mainly by the Länder. Q49 Q67
Three levels
  • Germany is divided into Bund, Länder und Kommunen (federation, states, municipalities). common sense Q64
  • The lowest political level is the Gemeinde (municipality); its head is the Bürgermeister. Q69 Q131
  • The Ordnungsamt (public order office) belongs to the municipal administration. Q56
Getting involved
  • They want to cut your bus line? Join or start a Bürgerinitiative (citizens' initiative). common sense Q134
  • Ehrenamtlich = working voluntarily and unpaid in clubs and associations (Vereine und Verbände). common sense Q132
