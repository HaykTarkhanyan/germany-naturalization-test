In 60 seconds
  • The national symbol is the eagle: coat of arms picture 1, and the Bundesadler in the Bundestag.
  • Flag: black-red-gold. Anthem starts: 'Einigkeit und Recht und Freiheit'.
  • Bundesrepublik Deutschland: about 84 million people, 9 neighbours.
Symbols
  • The coat of arms is the black eagle (picture 1). The Bundesadler also hangs in the Bundestag's plenary hall. Q21 Q29 Q216
  • The flag is schwarz-rot-gold (black-red-gold). common sense Q214
  • The anthem begins 'Einigkeit und Recht und Freiheit' (unity and justice and freedom). Q40
The country
  • Full name: Bundesrepublik Deutschland, not 'Bundesstaat Deutschland' (federal state is what Germany is, federal republic is its name). Population: about 84 million. Q212 Q213
  • The 9 neighbours: Denmark (Dänemark), Poland (Polen), Czech Republic (Tschechien), Austria (Österreich), Switzerland (Schweiz), France, Luxembourg, Belgium, Netherlands. In each 'Nachbarland' question exactly one option is on this list. common sense Q222 Q223 Q227 Q229 Q233
  • Austria (Österreich) has a large German-speaking population. common sense Q225
Watch out
  • In the Bundestag hangs the Bundesadler (federal eagle). The option Reichsadler (the old empire's eagle) is wrong. Q216
