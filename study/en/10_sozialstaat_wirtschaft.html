10 Sozialstaat, Sozialversicherung, Wirtschaft

Welfare state, social insurance, economy · Lesson and all exam questions

11 questions · about 1.1 of your 33 exam questions come from this topic · 6 to memorize, 5 common sense

If you have a job in Germany, social insurance comes out of your pay automatically. That is how the welfare state is paid for.

In 60 secondsSocial market economySocial insuranceWhat the state pays forWatch outAll 11 exam questions of this topic
In 60 seconds
  • The economic system is the soziale Marktwirtschaft: a free market, but the state ensures social balance.
  • Social insurance is paid automatically, by employers and employees together.
  • Life insurance is not social insurance, and VAT is not taken from your salary.
Social market economy
  • The economy is a soziale Marktwirtschaft: it follows supply and demand, but the state ensures social balance. Q50 Q171
Social insurance
  • It is financed by Sozialabgaben (social contributions), paid by employers and employees together, and automatically if you have a permanent job. Q35 Q99 Q97
  • It includes health insurance (which creates social security), pension, unemployment and long-term care insurance (Pflegeversicherung). Q36 Q45
What the state pays for
  • The state builds roads and schools, pays Kindergeld, supports museums and athletes, but it does not pay holidays for everyone. common sense Q46 Q47
Watch out
  • Not part of the statutory social insurance: life insurance (Lebensversicherung). Q100
  • An employee does not pay Umsatzsteuer (VAT) out of her salary. Wage tax (Lohnsteuer) and social contributions, yes. Q285
