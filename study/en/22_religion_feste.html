State and religion
  • In Germany state and religious communities are separate from each other. common sense Q33
  • Religious tolerance: everyone can believe what they want. common sense Q292
  • Christianity shaped European and German culture. common sense Q295
  • Your tax return asks whether you belong to a church because there is a church tax, linked to income tax and wage tax. Q291
Customs through the year
  • Advent (Adventszeit) = the last four weeks before Christmas. At Christmas people decorate a fir tree (Tannenbaum). Q296 Q271
  • At Easter people paint eggs. Pfingsten (Pentecost) is a Christian holiday. Q293 Q294
  • Colourful costumes and masks: Rosenmontag (carnival). Q264

Frage 295 · common sense
