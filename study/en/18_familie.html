18 Familie, Ehe, Kinder

Family, marriage, children · Lesson and all exam questions

16 questions · about 1.6 of your 33 exam questions come from this topic · 8 to memorize, 8 common sense

German family law in one line: adults decide for themselves, and children are protected, even from their own parents.

In 60 seconds
  • One marriage at a time. You marry at the Standesamt; a divorce needs a lawyer and usually a separation year.
  • Parents raise their children, and hitting a child is punishable.
  • Help with bringing up children: the Jugendamt, which can even take a child out of a violent family.
  • Adult at 18. Pregnant women get Mutterschutz; Elterngeld needs an application.
Partners and marriage
  • Adults of any combination may live together as a couple, but Anne (13) and Tim (25) may not: she is a child. common sense Q245
  • You may be married to only one partner at a time, so a man married to two women is not allowed. common sense Q252 Q272
  • To get married, you go first to the Standesamt (registry office). Q265
  • Divorce usually comes after the Trennungsjahr: the spouses live separately for at least a year (it does not mean the divorce takes a year). You need a lawyer. Q254 Q275
Children
  • Bringing up children is mainly the task of the parents. common sense Q248 Q249
  • Hitting a child can be punished. common sense Q251
  • Problems with bringing up your children? The Jugendamt (youth welfare office) helps. It can take a child who is beaten or starved out of the family. It does not pay Kindergeld (the Familienkasse does). Q255 Q273 Q258
  • You are an adult (volljährig) at 18. Parents must respect the decisions of their adult daughter, for example about her boyfriend. Q246 Q267
Babies
  • The legal protection shortly before and after birth is Mutterschutz. Q247
  • For Elterngeld (parental allowance) you apply at the Elterngeldstelle. It does not come automatically or through the health insurance. Q241

Frage 254 · memorize
In Deutschland dürfen Ehepaare sich scheiden lassen. Meistens müssen sie dazu das "Trennungsjahr" einhalten. Was bedeutet das?
In Germany married couples may get divorced. Usually they first have to observe the "separation year" (Trennungsjahr). What does that mean?
✓ Die Ehegatten führen mindestens ein Jahr getrennt ihr eigenes Leben. Danach ist die Scheidung möglich. (The spouses live their own lives separately for at least one year. After that, divorce is possible.)

Frage 258 · memorize
Was darf das Jugendamt in Deutschland?
What is the youth welfare office (Jugendamt) in Germany allowed to do?
✓ Es kann ein Kind, das geschlagen wird oder hungern muss, aus der Familie nehmen. (It can take a child who is beaten or has to go hungry out of the family.)
Frage 267 · common sense
Eine junge Frau in Deutschland, 22 Jahre alt, lebt mit ihrem Freund zusammen. Die Eltern der Frau finden das nicht gut, weil ihnen der Freund nicht gefällt. Was können die Eltern tun?
A young woman in Germany, 22 years old, lives with her boyfriend. Her parents don't approve because they don't like the boyfriend. What can the parents do?
✓ Sie müssen die Entscheidung der volljährigen Tochter respektieren. (They must respect the decision of their adult daughter.)
