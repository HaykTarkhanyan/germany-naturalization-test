11 Nationalsozialismus und Zweiter Weltkrieg

National Socialism and World War II · Lesson and all exam questions

15 questions · about 1.5 of your 33 exam questions come from this topic · 8 to memorize, 7 common sense

Most answers in this topic hang on four years: 1933, 1938, 1939 and 1945.

In 60 seconds1933: dictatorship1938: the pogrom1939-1945: the warWatch outAll 15 exam questions of this topic
In 60 seconds
  • The Nazis ruled 1933-1945; Hitler became chancellor in 1933 and set up a dictatorship.
  • 9 November 1938: synagogues and Jewish shops were destroyed.
  • World War II: 1939-1945. It ended in Europe on 8 May 1945 with Germany's unconditional surrender.
  • Stauffenberg: the attempt on Hitler's life on 20 July 1944.
1933: dictatorship
  • The Nazis under Hitler were in power from 1933 to 1945; Hitler became Reichskanzler in 1933. Q152 Q155 Q156
  • They built a dictatorship: the 'Third Reich' was a Diktatur. common sense Q157 Q158
  • There were no free elections, parties were banned, and the state's policy was state racism. common sense Q159 Q170 Q161
1938: the pogrom
  • On 9 November 1938, Nazis and their followers destroyed Jewish shops and synagogues. Q163 Q164
1939-1945: the war
  • World War II lasted from 1939 to 1945. common sense Q160
  • Claus Schenk Graf von Stauffenberg is known for the assassination attempt on Hitler on 20 July 1944. Q162
  • The war ended in Europe on 8 May 1945 (the year to remember: 1945) with Germany's unconditional surrender. Q153 Q154 Q179
Watch out
  • 'Was gab es nicht in der NS-Zeit?' The answer is free elections. Press censorship, arbitrary arrests and persecution of Jews all did exist. Q159
