Fired unfairly? Germany has a whole court just for work: the Arbeitsgericht.

In 60 seconds
  • Most people work for a company or public authority.
  • Gewerkschaften (unions) represent employees; inside a company the Betriebsrat helps.
  • Quitting: mind the Kündigungsfrist. Unfair dismissal: Kündigungsschutzklage at the Arbeitsgericht.
  • Good training gives the best chances, and you keep learning all your life.
Working
  • Most workers are employed by a company or a public authority (Firma oder Behörde), not self-employed and not mostly in small family businesses. Q23
  • Gewerkschaften (unions) represent employees (Arbeitnehmer). common sense Q101 Q135
  • Inside a company, the Betriebsrat (works council) helps employees with problems with the employer. Q286
Quitting and being fired
  • If you want to end your job, respect the Kündigungsfrist (notice period). common sense Q287
  • Fired without good reason? Go to the Arbeitsgericht (labour court, responsible for conflicts at work) and file a Kündigungsschutzklage. Q136 Q137 Q138
Careers
  • The best chance of a well-paid job: be well trained (gut ausgebildet). common sense Q250
  • Work changes fast, so adults must keep learning after their training. common sense Q284
  • The BIZ (Berufsinformationszentrum) of the Federal Employment Agency helps with finding an apprenticeship (Lehrstelle). common sense Q259
