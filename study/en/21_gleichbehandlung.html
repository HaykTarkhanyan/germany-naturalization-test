21 Gleichbehandlung und Diskriminierung

Equal treatment and discrimination · Lesson and all exam questions

6 questions · about 0.6 of your 33 exam questions come from this topic · 1 to memorize, 5 common sense

The easiest topic: when a job applicant is turned down, the discrimination answer is the personal trait the question mentions. One exception to watch: long illness can be a legal reason for dismissal, pregnancy never.

In 60 seconds
  • Gleichbehandlung: nobody may be disadvantaged, for example because of a disability.
  • Discrimination = being turned down for who you are (mother, wheelchair, skin colour), not for missing skills.
The principle
  • Gleichbehandlung (equal treatment): nobody may be disadvantaged, for example because of a disability. common sense Q262
  • Two friends with dark skin are refused entry to a public pool: their right to Gleichbehandlung is violated, not freedom of opinion or of assembly. common sense Q281
Spot the discrimination
  • A woman may not be fired because she is having a child and her boss knows it. Being often late, doing private things at work, or even being ill for a long time and unable to work can be legal reasons. Q104
  • Turned down only because she is a mother, he uses a wheelchair or he has dark skin = discrimination. Turned down for no experience, weak language skills or high salary wishes = not discrimination. common sense Q277 Q278 Q289
